## Abstract

This Replicated Computational Results \(RCR\) Report accompanies our TOSEM article “Test Oracle Generation for REST APIs\.” In this work we introduce AGORA\+, a black\-box approach for automatically generating domain\-specific test oracles for REST APIs by detecting invariants—output properties that should always hold\. As part of this RCR, we provide a replication package \(available at https://doi\.org/10\.5281/zenodo\.12506791 \) that enables the full reproduction of our results and is designed to pave the way for future research\.
