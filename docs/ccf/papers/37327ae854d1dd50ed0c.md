## Abstract

Software development increasingly involves heterogeneous stakeholders, including diverse human contributors and AI\-powered agents\. This amplifies governance challenges around transparency, participation, and accountability, particularly in Open\-Source Software \(OSS\) projects where policies are usually implicit\. Building on our prior vision paper, we present a framework for defining and enforcing governance policies via a Domain\-Specific Language \(DSL\) and an executable decision engine\. The DSL captures scopes, roles and individuals \(with weighted participation and profiles\), policy composition and deadlines, and platform\-aware conditions\. The engine operationalizes policies over collaboration events and records votes, rationales, and timestamps for traceability\. We extend the core language with bindings for GitHub and GitLab and provide a form\-based editor to ease authoring\. We evaluate expressivity by formalizing existing GOVERNANCE\.md files from a curated dataset and demonstrate enactment by reproducing three governance policies from different GitHub repositories\.
